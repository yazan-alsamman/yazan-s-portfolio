# Brand Identity — Yazan Al Samman

## Brand Direction

### Keywords

- Intelligent
- Precise
- Cinematic
- Technical
- Experimental
- Premium
- Quietly futuristic
- Architectural
- Human
- Engineered

### Avoid

- Gaming aesthetic
- Overly saturated cyberpunk
- Generic AI stock imagery
- Excessive gradients
- "Hacker" stereotypes
- Decorative UI without purpose
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Template-like portfolio cards

### Usage

- Obsidian / Carbon: primary environment
- Cloud / White: primary typography
- Mist: secondary typography
- Electric Cyan: primary interactive/accent color
- AI Violet: secondary technology accent
- Signal Blue: links / selected states
- Precision Green: verified/success states
- Amber: warnings only

Do not use every accent simultaneously.

## Light Mode

A light theme may exist for accessibility/content readability, but the flagship landing experience should remain visually coherent with the dark technical identity.

Suggested light tokens:

```text
Background    #F5F7FA
Surface       #FFFFFF
Text          #10151C
Muted         #5F6874
Accent        #007F9A
Secondary     #6557D9
```

## Typography

Recommended:

- Display: `Space Grotesk` or another geometric modern display family
- Body: `Inter`
- Arabic: `IBM Plex Sans Arabic` or `Noto Sans Arabic`

Do not mix more than necessary.

Typography must support:
- Latin numerals,
- Arabic shaping,
- Arabic punctuation,
- bilingual navigation,
- long-form project descriptions.

## Photography Direction

The supplied portrait is a primary brand asset.

Treat the portrait as a professional editorial subject.

Possible treatments:
- cinematic rim light,
- controlled monochrome/duotone treatment,
- subtle depth separation,
- integration into the 3D environment,
- portrait revealed during scroll,
- portrait as a human anchor against abstract computational systems.

Do not distort the face or use aggressive AI manipulation.

## Motion Direction

Motion should feel:
- physically plausible,
- deliberate,
- smooth,
- restrained,
- responsive to scroll.

Avoid:
- constant bouncing,
- excessive particle noise,
- animation everywhere,
- motion that competes with text.

## Interaction Principle

Every major animation must have a purpose:
- reveal,
- orientation,
- hierarchy,
- storytelling,
- state feedback,
- spatial continuity.

If an animation does not improve one of these, remove it.
